About

Laura Brambilla

Your Reflector therapist & guide to alignment

More than twenty years of global experience — woven into one cohesive practice that bridges mind, body and energy.

Book a free call

Resilience is not about being unbreakable. It's about finding the light, wisdom and unshakable strength that comes from the cracks.

For over two decades I have lived and worked as a coach, entrepreneur, consultant and humanitarian worker across the globe — on projects in psychosocial support, education, peacebuilding and conflict resolution.

Originally from Italy, my anchor is now Beirut. Living here has taught me, in the most visceral way, that resilience isn't about being unbreakable — it's about the light that comes through the cracks. This is the core truth I bring into my work.

My education reflects a global path: studies in Italy and the United Kingdom, professional certifications in the United States and Malaysia, and teachings drawn from Indian and Japanese wisdom traditions.

This synthesis of Western education and Eastern philosophy forms the backbone of my integrative practice — a space where clients feel truly seen, safe, and empowered to embrace every part of their story, especially the fractured ones.
