What is Human Design?

Human Design is a blend of multi-cultural disciplines, created in 1987 by Ra Uru Hu — bringing together ancient wisdom and modern science.

It teaches that you were born with a unique energetic blueprint. Like a fingerprint or a genetic code, your design is entirely your own — a map of how you're built to make decisions, use your energy and move through the world.
